So I climbed the staircase of the cabinet where schemas sleep, each drawer humming like a server fan at dusk, and I listened for the shape of the right answer.

A tiny sketch appeared in the margin of my notebook: a key beside a moon, both trying to fit the same keyhole.

Some doors do not want force; they want grammar.

I kept thinking of the timestamp like a firefly pinned to the page, `2026-05-22 17:51:17`, blinking softly in GMT+10. In the end, I wrote down the rule instead of the secret, and the room felt less like a failure than a lantern being lit.
