About

30 years of master craftsmanship

Mark Dennerley brings a unique blend of artistic creativity and practical design skills to every project. Heritage specialist, master craftsman.

Mark Dennerley, owner of Melbourne Plastering & Building Maintenance, has a unique blend of 30 years experience, artistic creativity and practical design skills. He will take on your project as if it was his own — with your best interest at heart.

Mark strives for perfection and excellence in whatever project he works on. His keen eye for detail and skills have earned him respect from his customers and fellow tradesmen, who often refer to him as a master craftsman.

At Melbourne Plastering & Building Maintenance we have been providing solutions for customers' needs for 30 years. We love what we do — and we do it well.

Contact Mark to take the guesswork out of your next project. Put yourself at ease with Mark's friendly professional approach and expertise.

Services

What we do

Residential Plastering

Architecturally designed homes, square set finishes, renovations, extensions, and laser battening.

Heritage Plastering

Solid plastering, period restoration, ornamental cornicing and archways, skim coatings, ceiling roses, domes, barrel vaulted ceilings, stucco, and lathe and plaster repairs.

Commercial Plastering

Suspended ceilings — acoustic tile and plasterboard — metal stud wall installation, and solid plastering for commercial interiors.

Building Maintenance

Demolition, remodelling, general carpentry, painting, tiling, general repairs, and render repairs.
